# The status bar menu

After you sign in, the robot icon at the bottom of the window opens Copilot's menu. Click it to see where Copilot stands.

![Figure 16.4 - The Copilot status bar menu](images/ch16-status-bar-menu.png "width=50%")

*Figure 16.4 - The robot icon's menu after sign-in, showing the plan, credits, and Inline Suggestions*

## What the menu shows after sign-in

- **The plan name** at the top. A personal account on the free tier shows `Copilot Free`. If your plan shows something else, stop, and check which account you are signed in with, as the account lesson explained.
- **Credits**, with the share you have used and the date they reset.
- **Inline Suggestions**, with a status of Enabled. This is the switch for ghost text. Its submenu turns suggestions on or off.
- **Codebase Semantic Index** and **Session Sync**. These are extra features. They may show as out of date or not enabled. You can leave them alone for this course.

## Check Inline Suggestions

Inline Suggestions is enabled by default. Still, open the menu and check that it says Enabled before you go on. If it says anything else, open its submenu and turn it on, because the rest of this part of the course depends on ghost text.

## An example: suggestions off for one language

You are writing Markdown notes, and you do not want ghost text in them. Open the menu, and turn suggestions off for Markdown. Leave them on for Python, where you want them. The switch applies to one language, so your other files are not affected.

## Try it

Turn suggestions off everywhere, then on again. Watch the menu, so you know which state it is in.
