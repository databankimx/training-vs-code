# 22. When Things Go Wrong

Most of the time VS Code does what you expect. When it does not, the cause is usually one of a few things: an extension, a setting, a shortcut, or a folder you have not trusted yet. This chapter shows how to find which one, and how to get back to a working editor without losing your work.

The first rule is to stay calm and look before you change anything. Most fixes are small once you know where the problem is.
