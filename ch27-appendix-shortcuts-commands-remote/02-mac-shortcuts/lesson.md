# Mac shortcuts

For most keys, the translation is mechanical. Ctrl becomes Cmd, Alt becomes Option, and Shift and the letter keys stay the same. So Ctrl+Shift+P becomes Cmd+Shift+P, and Alt+Up becomes Option+Up.

The exceptions are the keys where Mac VS Code keeps Ctrl, uses a different key, or needs the fn key. The Windows table marks the uncertain ones with "verify", and these are the ones to check on a Mac before you rely on them.

## The rules

- **Ctrl becomes Cmd** for most editing and file keys, such as Cmd+S, Cmd+Z, and Cmd+Shift+P.
- **Alt becomes Option** for line moves, cursors, and word wrap, such as Option+Up and Option+Z.
- **Function keys need fn.** On many Mac keyboards, F5 and F12 need fn held, unless the system setting that uses F-keys as standard function keys is on.

## Exceptions to check

- **Alt+Left and Alt+Right** (go back and forward) are Ctrl+- and Ctrl+Shift+- on Mac.
- **Ctrl+H** (replace) is Cmd+Option+F on Mac.
- **Ctrl+Y** (redo) is Cmd+Shift+Z on Mac.
- **Ctrl+G** (go to line) stays Ctrl+G on Mac.
- **Ctrl+` and Ctrl+Space** stay Ctrl on Mac.
- **Ctrl+I** (hover, after Ctrl+K) is Cmd+K, then Cmd+I on Mac.
- **Delete** on a Mac keyboard deletes backward. Forward delete is fn+Delete.

> **The Mac column is a guide.** It was written from the usual mapping, not checked on a Mac. Check every entry marked "verify" before the appendix is published.
