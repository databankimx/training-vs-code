# Accepting, dismissing, and cycling

With a suggestion showing, you choose what happens to it. There are four moves to learn:

- **Tab** accepts the whole suggestion.
- **Ctrl+Right** accepts one word at a time.
- **Esc** dismisses it, and the file stays as it was.
- **Alt+]** and **Alt+[** move to the next or previous suggestion, when Copilot has offered more than one.

## Example: accept, then undo

Type `def multiply(a, b):` on a new line in `calculator.py`. When `return a * b` appears in grey, press Tab. The line is now in the file. Press {{Ctrl+Z}}. The line disappears, and the file is back to what you typed. Accepting a suggestion is one change, so one undo removes it.

## Example: accept one word at a time

Sometimes Copilot gets the first part of a line right and the rest wrong. With a suggestion showing, press Ctrl+Right to take one word, check it, and press it again for the next word. Stop when the line is what you want, then press Esc to dismiss the rest.

## Example: cycle to another suggestion

Copilot sometimes offers two or more versions of the same code. Press Alt+] to see the next one, and Alt+[ to go back. Choose the version that matches the function's name and purpose, not the one that is shortest.

## Dismissing by typing on

If you do not want a suggestion, keep typing. Ghost text disappears when what you type no longer matches it. Press Esc when you want it gone at once.
