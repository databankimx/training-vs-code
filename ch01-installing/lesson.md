# 1. Installing and the First Launch

This chapter gets VS Code onto your computer and open for the first time. You will see the Welcome tab, the workspace trust prompt, and the settings for the color theme and the display language. You will also meet the sign-in options, and one caution about Settings Sync that is worth reading before you turn it on.

Most of this chapter is reading. The last step is a checklist, and it is the one to finish, because the later chapters assume these settings are in place.
