# Chapter summary

You can find text in a file, replace it exactly, search a whole folder, and move between the places that matter. Keep these in mind:

- {{Ctrl+F}} finds text in the open file, and {{Ctrl+H}} replaces it. Turn on Match Whole Word with {{Alt+W}} when you replace a name.
- {{Ctrl+Shift+F}} searches the folder. Narrow it with the include and exclude boxes.
- {{F12}} goes to a definition, {{Shift+F12}} lists the uses of a name, and {{Alt+F12}} shows the definition in a small box.
- {{Ctrl+G}} goes to a line, {{Ctrl+Shift+O}} goes to a symbol, and {{Alt+Left}} and {{Alt+Right}} go back and forward.

```quiz
Which key opens Find and Replace in the open file?
○ Ctrl+F
→ Ctrl+F opens Find only. Ctrl+H opens Replace as well.
◉ Ctrl+H
→ Ctrl+H opens Replace, with a second box for the new text.
○ Ctrl+Shift+H
→ Ctrl+Shift+H replaces across the folder, not the open file.
```

```quiz
Which key goes to the definition of the name under the cursor?
○ Shift+F12
→ Shift+F12 lists the uses of a name. It does not jump to its definition.
○ Alt+F12
→ Alt+F12 shows the definition in a small box, and does not move you there.
◉ F12
→ F12 jumps to the definition. Alt+Left takes you back.
```

```quiz
Which key moves you back to the place you were before your last jump?
○ Ctrl+Z
→ Ctrl+Z undoes a change to the text. It does not move the cursor back.
○ Alt+Right
→ Alt+Right goes forward again, to the place you came from.
◉ Alt+Left
→ Alt+Left goes back, and Alt+Right goes forward.
```
