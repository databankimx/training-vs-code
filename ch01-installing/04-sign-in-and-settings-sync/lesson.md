# Signing in and Settings Sync

You can sign in to VS Code with a Microsoft account or a GitHub account. Signing in is optional for most of the course, but some features ask for it, and the GitHub chapters need a GitHub account. Chapter 2 is where you create the one you will use for practice.

To sign in, select the account icon at the bottom of the activity bar on the left. The menu offers the sign-in options. Choose the account the course asks for, and follow the prompts in your browser.

Signing in to VS Code is separate from the sign-in that Git uses to talk to GitHub. Chapter 2 covers the Git side, so you do not need to sort it out today.

## Settings Sync

After you sign in, VS Code can offer to turn on Settings Sync. It copies your settings, keyboard shortcuts, snippets, extensions, and window layout to your account, and it applies them on any other computer where you sign in to that account.

> **Caution:** leave Settings Sync off for this course. The course changes settings on purpose, and a copy of someone else's settings can make your screen look different from the pictures and from the lessons. Settings can also hold paths, names, and server addresses, and you may not want those copied to another machine. If you have already turned it on, open the account menu and choose Settings Sync to turn it off.

If you have already signed in to a personal account and turned on sync, that is fine to leave as it is for your own use. Just keep it off on the course's practice settings, so that what you learn here matches what you see.
